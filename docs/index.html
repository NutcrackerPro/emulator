Keep the Nutcracker launcher running on the Mac that holds your VM.

01 / FULLSCREEN

Just your Windows display.

Use Fullscreen in the console to hide its toolbar, labels, and website controls.

02 / KEYBOARD

Your Windows shortcuts.

Use Ctrl for Windows shortcuts. The console also has Start, Run, and Ctrl + Alt + Del buttons.

03 / PRIVATE MACHINE

Your desktop stays on your Mac.

Sign in to your local console with your Nutcracker account. Your Windows files and Steam account stay in your VM.

If Windows does not open

Start Launch Nutcracker.command on your Mac and start your Windows VM, then choose Sign in to Windows again. Setup instructions are in the project guide. GitHub hosts this launch page; your Mac runs Windows.

Leaving fullscreen

Hold Esc for two seconds, or press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + M. Some shortcuts are reserved by your Mac or browser.
